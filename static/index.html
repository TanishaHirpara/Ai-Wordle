💡 How the AI Solvers Work

▼
🎲 Random Baseline
Picks any possible word uniformly at random from remaining candidates. Establishes the performance floor.
📊 Frequency Heuristic
Scores words by how common their letters are among remaining candidates, greedily choosing words with high-frequency letters.
🛡️ Candidate Reduction
Minimax heuristic: for every candidate guess, simulates all possible feedback patterns and picks the word that minimises the worst-case surviving words.
🧠 Shannon Entropy
Information gain: computes \(H = -\sum p_i \log_2(p_i)\) across feedback distributions to pick the guess expected to maximise revealed information (bits).
